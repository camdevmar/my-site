Jane runs a small bakery and kept her recipes on sticky notes, scraps of paper and three different phones.

She wanted one place where she could scale a recipe up for a wedding order, or down for a Tuesday, without doing math with flour on her hands.

I built her a simple app that stores every recipe, rescales ingredients with one tap, and works offline in the kitchen.

(This is an example project. Delete the folder `projects/000-example-person` when you add your first real one.)
